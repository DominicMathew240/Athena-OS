Questions

What is Athena OS?

Athena OS is open-source vision AI for operations teams. Show it a photo or a short video and ask a question in plain words. It counts, spots and explains — then tells you what to do next.

Do I need to write code?

No. Pick a ready-made check like parking occupancy, or write your question the way you would ask a colleague. No data science.

Where does it run?

On your own server. Apache-2.0. Pictures can stay on your network. There is no Athena cloud on this page.

Which AI does it use?

It ships with the open Qwen vision-language model. You can also plug in any AI that speaks the OpenAI-compatible API, including OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, vLLM, Ollama and LM Studio.

How does this relate to Inspec-Ther?

Inspec-Ther is the solar PV thermal inspection product at inspec-ther.com. The in-house vision stack behind it is Athena Vision (Segment and Detect). Athena OS on this page is the open-source toolkit, not the hosted Inspec-Ther app.
