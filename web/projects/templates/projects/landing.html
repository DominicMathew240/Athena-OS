    03 How it works

    From photo to report in three steps.

    1. 01

      Say what to check

      Choose a ready-made check like Parking occupancy, or write your question the way you would ask a colleague.

      Corrosion checkParkingSafety gearYour own question…
    2. 02

      Add photos or a video

      Drop in up to six photos, or a short clip. Athena takes the still frames it needs by itself.

    3. 03

      Read the report

      A verdict, counts, numbered areas on the picture, and practical next steps. Print it, share it, or rate it.

    04 Your AI, your rules

    Open-source Qwen by default. Any AI you like, when you want.

    Athena ships with the open Qwen vision-language model running on your own server, so pictures never leave your network. Each person can also plug in their own AI with an address and a key — anything that speaks the OpenAI-compatible API.
