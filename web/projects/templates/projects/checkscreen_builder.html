Set up your check screen

A check screen is a simple page where someone uploads a picture and a computer model points out what it found. A person then decides what to do. Choose how it looks and behaves here, then try it on the right.

Which screen?

Pick a screen you already made, or start a new one.

The basics

Only a model that a person has already approved can be used. Approving happens in the training area, after reading how well the model did on test pictures.

People see this at the top of the screen, for example “Bag check, lane 4”.

Lower-case letters, numbers and dashes, such as lane-4. Leave it empty and one is made from the title. It cannot be changed later.

What to look for

Show the computer’s findings for these things

Anything you leave unticked is hidden from the screen, even if the model finds it.

Kinds of picture people can upload

Other kinds of file are turned away with a clear message.

How sure must the computer be?

Flag more things Flag fewer things

To the left, the screen points out more things, including some that are not really there. To the right, it points out only what it is quite sure about, and may miss real things. The number is the computer’s own guess, not a measured accuracy.

What the screen says

Shown before anyone has decided (if a person must always decide), and again after someone rejects the result or asks for a second look.

What the person checking can do

Decision buttons they see

“Confirm” means they agree with the result; “Reject” means they disagree; “Needs a second look” passes it on.
