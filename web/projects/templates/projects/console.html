Get started

Follow these steps in order. The list updates itself as you work.

You have no project open yet. Start by creating one.

    Labels

    A label is the name of something you want to find, such as “knife” or “laptop”. You can add, rename, reorder, or remove labels at any time. Renaming a label also renames it on boxes you have already drawn.

      Pictures

      Add JPEG, PNG, or WebP pictures to this project. Each one is checked and then stored on this Athena server.

      Auto-suggest boxes

      The computer looks at pictures that have no boxes yet and suggests boxes using your label names. Every suggestion is marked “Needs your review” and does not count until a person accepts it. This works best when labels name things you can see, such as “knife” or “laptop”.

      These pictures have not been checked for usage rights. Do not treat them as cleared for sharing or for training a model.

      Make a training set

      A training set is your labelled pictures, packaged so a training tool can learn from them. You save it as a snapshot: a frozen copy that never changes. If you add pictures or change boxes later, those changes go into your next snapshot, so you can always get back to exactly what you saved.

        Fix these first

            Save a snapshot

            Only boxes you have accepted go in. Boxes that still say “Needs your review” are left out.

            Advanced: choose how pictures are divided into groups

            Training tools learn from one group of pictures and are checked on others they have never seen. By default Athena puts about 80% of your pictures in Training (the model learns from these), 10% in Checking (used to see how it is doing while it learns; some tools call this “validation”) and 10% in Final test (kept back for an honest score). Identical copies of a picture always stay together in one group. You only need to change this if your training tool asks for something different.

            Your snapshots

              Athena does not check whether you have the right to use these pictures for training. Make sure you do before you share a download.

              Teach the computer

              Here the computer learns from one of your snapshots, and then you decide whether to trust what it learned. It always goes in this order:

              1. You choose a snapshot and press “Start teaching”.
              2. The computer learns from the pictures in the Training group. This can take a while.
              3. It is checked on the Final test pictures it has never seen, and you get a plain report.
              4. You decide. Finishing never switches a model on by itself. A model is used only after a person approves it.

                      How to mark items
                      Pick a label, then drag across the picture to draw a box around an item. Drag inside a box to move it; drag a corner square to resize it. With a box selected, the arrow keys move it by 1% and Shift+arrow resizes it. Press Ctrl/Cmd+Z to undo and Shift+Ctrl/Cmd+Z to redo while the picture is focused. Undo history lasts until you close this window. You can also type a box position in the form below. Every change is saved as you make it.
