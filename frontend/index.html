Train AI on all your data.

Athena OS is an open-source, self-hosted no-code AI training and visual inspection platform across photos, video, audio, handwriting, documents, signals, and spreadsheets. Apache-2.0.

Capabilities & checks

  • Computer Vision & Visual Inspection (corrosion, defects, safety gear, snapshots; 18 of 65 vision tasks locally verified)
  • Acoustic Clip Classification (WAV + ONNX/torch via audio-wav-cnn)
  • Handwriting OCR (TrOCR fine-tuning with CER/WER evaluation)
  • Spreadsheets, Text Classification & Windowed Signals (sklearn trainers)
  • Roadmap Waves 3–4: Human Pose / Gestures & Cross-modal Fusion

No. Pick an outcome recipe or ready-made check, label samples visually, and inspect held-out metrics in plain words.
