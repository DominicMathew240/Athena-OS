Train AI on all your data.

Athena OS is an open-source, self-hosted no-code AI training and visual inspection platform across photos, video, audio, handwriting, documents, motion, and spreadsheets. Apache-2.0.

Capabilities & checks

  • Computer Vision & Visual Inspection (corrosion, defects, safety gear, snapshots)
  • Acoustic Event & Equipment Fault Detection
  • Handwriting OCR (TrOCR) & Document Extraction
  • Spreadsheets, Time-Series & Human Pose / Gestures
  • Cross-modal Fusion (Audio + Vibration + Telemetry)

What is Athena OS?

Do I need to write code?

No. Pick an outcome recipe or ready-made check, label samples visually, and inspect held-out metrics in plain words.
