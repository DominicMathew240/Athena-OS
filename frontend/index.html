Train AI on all your data.

Athena OS is an open-source, self-hosted no-code AI training and visual inspection platform across photos, video, audio, handwriting, documents, signals, and spreadsheets. Apache-2.0.

Capabilities & checks

  • Computer Vision & Visual Inspection (corrosion, defects, safety gear, parking & entrance snapshots)
  • Acoustic Clip Classification (WAV motor & bearing fault detection)
  • Handwriting OCR (TrOCR fine-tuning for maintenance logs & forms)
  • Spreadsheets, Text Sorting & Sensor Signal Classification
  • Multi-Signal Fusion & Offline Edge Deployment

What is Athena OS?

Athena OS is an open-source, self-hosted AI training and inspection platform across vision, audio, handwriting, documents, signals, and spreadsheets.

Do I need to write code?

No. Pick an outcome recipe or ready-made check, label samples visually, and inspect held-out metrics in plain words.
